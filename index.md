# Odolog Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Odolog is an Android app for keeping track of a vehicle's services, fuel, renewals and running costs. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

## In short

- Everything you enter stays on your phone. Odolog has no account and no server, and the developer never receives your records.
- Odolog has no ads, analytics or tracking.
- Your data leaves your phone only when you choose to: a backup file or export you save, a PDF you share, Google Drive backup if you turn it on, or an email you send.

## What Odolog stores on your phone

- Your vehicles (type, make and model, year, plate number, nickname) and their odometer readings.
- The services, fill-ups, renewal documents, other costs and reminders you add.
- Photos you attach, such as receipts and documents. Odolog copies each one into its own storage, so it stays in the app even if you delete it from your gallery.
- Your settings, such as units, currency and reminder time.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.

## When your data leaves your phone

Only when you do one of these:

- **Back up to a file or export to CSV.** Odolog writes the file to the place you pick in Android's file picker. What happens to it after that is up to you.
- **Share the service history PDF** (Pro). It goes only to the app or person you share it with.
- **Turn on Google Drive backup** (Pro, off unless you turn it on). Once a day Odolog uploads a backup of your records and photos to your own Google Drive as "Odolog backup.zip", replacing the previous one. It uses Google's limited Drive permission, which lets Odolog see only the files it created, not anything else in your Drive. The upload goes straight from your phone to Google; the developer can't see your Drive. Turning the feature off stops the uploads. The last backup stays in your Drive until you delete it.
- **Send feedback.** This opens your email app with a message to odolog.app@gmail.com. If you send it, the developer receives your email address and what you wrote, and uses them only to reply to you.
- **Android's own device backup.** If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Odolog's data in your phone's backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. Google and Android handle this, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

## Purchases

Odolog Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment. Odolog only learns whether you own Pro, and never sees your payment details. The Google Play Billing library in the app may send diagnostic information about purchases to Google.

Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to purchases and to connecting Google Drive.

## Notifications

Reminders are worked out on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere to schedule them. You can turn notifications off in Odolog's settings or in Android's settings.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**, to remind you when a service or renewal is due.
- **Internet**, only for Google Play purchases and Google Drive backup.
- **Running in the background and after a restart**, so the daily reminder check and Drive backup happen on time.

Odolog doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone. Photos are chosen with Android's photo picker, which gives Odolog only the photos you pick.

## Deleting your data

- Delete any record, or a vehicle and everything recorded for it, inside the app.
- Uninstalling Odolog deletes everything it stored on your phone.
- Backup files and exports you saved are yours to keep or delete.
- To remove the Drive backup, delete "Odolog backup.zip" from your Google Drive.

## Children

Odolog isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.
